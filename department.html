Department of Computer Science - COMSATS University Islamabad, Wah Campus

The Department of Computer Science at COMSATS University Islamabad, Wah Campus is one of the most active and reputable departments in the region. It was established to prepare students for careers in software development, artificial intelligence, cyber security, and data science. The department offers modern computing laboratories, a dedicated research centre, and an experienced faculty that works closely with students on both academic projects and industry collaborations. Along with regular classes, students take part in coding competitions, final year projects, and internship programs with local software houses.

Degree Programs Offered

  • BS Computer Science (4 years)
  • BS Software Engineering (4 years)
  • BS Artificial Intelligence (4 years)
  • MS Computer Science (2 years)
  • PhD Computer Science
